L28 Feed the World Classic look

The Works, ammonia from natural gas

Brief

  • Natural gas in, ammonia out. The world's best plants use 28 gigajoules a tonne. The average is 36.6.
  • The whole plant is built. Tune it: the reformer exit temperature, the steam, the exchanger, the shift, the CO₂ recovery, the purge and the nitrogen. Burn what the purge carries.
  • First knob: raise the reformer exit from 800 to about 880 °C and watch the methane slip fall. The Works was handed over badly trimmed (steam at 3.5 per carbon, a 400 m² feed-effluent exchanger, a 4 % purge): trim the steam, enlarge the exchanger and close the purge for the top quartile. The calibrated best is 32.2 GJ per tonne: the energy our reference engineer reached on this same Works (31.9) plus 1 %; it is not a book number, and it is a little tighter than the 33 GJ top quartile. The Works is already paid for: nothing is for sale here and nothing counts as spent. Call it: predict before your first commission. The answer box locks when you commission; the printout marks your call right or wrong.

Goal

  • Ammonia at least 95 t/day
  • Energy at most 36.6 GJ per tonne of NH₃ (IFA 2008 survey average)

Stars

  1. At least 95 t/day of NH₃ at no more than 36.6 GJ per tonne
  2. Top-quartile energy: 33 GJ per tonne or less
  3. Energy at or under the calibrated best: 32.2 GJ per tonne (our reference engineer's 31.9 plus 1 %; the top quartile is 33)

If it fails

The printout names one of these when a goal is missed:

  • Above the survey average.
  • Not enough ammonia.
Coach steps (hints that show how to build)
  1. Call it first: the share of the methane the reformer converts at your exit temperature.steam reforming is endothermic and makes more molecules, so heat and low pressure push it toward the products.
  2. Raise the reformer exit temperature toward 880 °C and read the methane slip.every unconverted methane is a loss of feedstock and an inert in the loop.
  3. Open the purge a little and set the nitrogen so the fresh gas is three hydrogen to one nitrogen.the loop can only consume hydrogen and nitrogen three to one; the surplus of either has to leave through the purge.
  4. Commission and read the energy.the energy is the natural gas fed, the fuel the furnace needs, the steam, the compressors and the CO₂ solvent, less what the boiler gives back.
  5. Compare your gigajoules per tonne with the IFA average (36.6), the top quartile (33) and the best plants (28).the thermodynamic minimum is far lower; the gap is heat you throw away.
  6. Ask Why? and see what the purge costs and what it gives back as fuel.the purge is hydrogen and methane; burnt in the furnace it replaces natural gas, burnt too much it wastes hydrogen you paid to make.
