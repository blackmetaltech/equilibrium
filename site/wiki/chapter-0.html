The prologue. A running ammonia loop at 02:40, one pump trip and one dial. The cooler is slow: turn it late and the interlock trips the plant.

Night Shift

L00 Night Shift

The ammonia loop, 02:40

Brief

  • Last night shift. She is running sweet, but watch the CW PUMP lamp.
  • When the CW PUMP lamp lights, you have about half a minute.

Goal

  • Get the plant through the night shift

Stars

  1. Get through the night without a trip
  2. Keep the catalyst lit: do not set the cooler below 370 °C (643 K). Colder than that, the iron stops working and the converter goes out.
  3. Turn the cooler within 10 s of the pump trip

Steps

  1. Tap the converter ….the Equilibrium Window shows where the converter sits on its curve.
  2. Bring the converter inlet down: turn the TEMPERATURE dial on the cooler ….less heat in at the inlet puts the converter lower on its curve, and the beam comes back level. The TEMPERATURE dial is what you ask of the cooler; with the cooling water failing it cannot reach it all, so the stream temperature you read stays above the dial.

What it teaches

The cooling water pump tripped and the cooler could no longer take the heat off the converter inlet. The converter outlet was … °C before the trip and peaked at … °C after it. One dial, one new place on the curve.
