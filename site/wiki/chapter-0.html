Prologue · Night Shift

What it teaches. A running ammonia loop at 02:40, one pump trip and one dial. The cooler is slow: turn it late and the interlock trips the plant. You learn to watch the Equilibrium Window and act within seconds.

1 job: L00. Site: The gate. All of its jobs are drawn in the new look: a 3D hall and lab.

Night Shift

L00 Night Shift New look

The ammonia loop, 02:40

Brief

  • Last night shift. She is running sweet, but watch the CW PUMP lamp.
  • When the CW PUMP lamp lights, you have about half a minute.

Goal

  • Get the plant through the night shift

Stars

  1. Get through the night without a trip
  2. Keep the catalyst lit: do not set the cooler below 370 °C (643 K). Colder than that, the iron stops working and the converter goes out.
  3. Turn the cooler within 10 s of the pump trip

If it fails

The interlock tripped the loop. The cooler came too late: it moves slowly, so turn it as soon as the CW PUMP lamp lights.
