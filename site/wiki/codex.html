Chemical equilibrium

A reaction stops changing when the reaction quotient Q equals the equilibrium constant K(T).

K depends on temperature only: pressure, inerts and concentration move the position (Q), not K.

Simplified: Ideal-gas activities at the plant pressure; standard state 1 bar.

Extent of reaction

One number ξ says how far a reaction has gone: every species changes by its stoichiometric coefficient times ξ.

With ξ the atom balance holds by construction, and a reactor with several reactions is one number per reaction instead of one per species.

Simplified: Each reaction is written with whole-number coefficients; side reactions you do not list do not happen.
