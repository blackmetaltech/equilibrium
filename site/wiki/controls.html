Classic look (all other chapters, and the drafting table)

Place parts from the tray, drag between ports to pipe them, click a part to open its faceplate, turn the key from DESIGN to COMMISSION to run the plant. The notebook holds the brief and the Call it line. On a pad: LB / RB switch drawers, the D-pad moves focus, A presses, B goes back, Y steps the clock, Back changes speed.
